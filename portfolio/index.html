About

I like problems where correctness is visible. A calculator either gives the right answer or it does not; a gallery either loads the photo or shows an honest placeholder. That constraint is what keeps me interested — it forces the small decisions (error states, keyboard support, timeouts) that make software feel finished.

Most of my work is plain Python and plain JavaScript, with FastAPI on the backend when a project needs one. I’m currently deepening my data-structures and algorithms practice in Python, one topic a week, and documenting it as I go.
